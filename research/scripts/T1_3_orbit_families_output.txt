5. GROUND-TRACK ANCHORS
==============================================================================
ISS: Earth rotates 23.31 deg during one ISS period (92.97 min); with J2 node drift the westward shift is 23.62 deg/orbit
     (master-doc anchor says ~22.5 deg; the arithmetic gives ~23.3-23.6 deg -- use 23.5 deg)
ISS max ground-track latitude = inclination = 51.63 deg N/S
GPS: period exactly half a sidereal day -> 2 orbits per sidereal day -> ground track repeats every sidereal day (4 min earlier each solar day)
GEO: shift per orbit = 360.0134 deg -> a fixed point on the equator (residual is the J2 node term, meaningless at i=0)
Molniya: period 719.59 min vs half sidereal day 718.03 min -> 2 revs per day; ground track repeats daily, apogee alternates between two longitudes ~180 deg apart
SSO: plane rotates +0.9856 deg/day eastward = the Sun rate, so local solar time of the ascending node stays fixed

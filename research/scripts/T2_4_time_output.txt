4. What one second of time error does to a ground track
==============================================================================
omega_e = 7.2921150e-05 rad/s -> sidereal day = 2pi/omega_e = 86164.1006 s = 23h 56m 4.1006s
Earth rotation = 360 deg / 86164.1 s = 0.0041781 deg/s = 15.0411 arcsec/s
Equatorial surface speed = R_E * omega_e = 0.46510 km/s  -> 1 s of GMST/clock error shifts the ground track 465.1 m at the equator
   at latitude  0.0 deg:   465.1 m per second of error (x cos(lat))
   at latitude 30.6 deg:   400.3 m per second of error (x cos(lat))
   at latitude 51.6 deg:   288.9 m per second of error (x cos(lat))
   at latitude 60.0 deg:   232.6 m per second of error (x cos(lat))
Ground-track error from using the WRONG time scale for GMST (error = offset * 0.4651 km at the equator):
   ignore UT1-UTC today (DUT1 = +0.00243 s, finals.daily MJD 61284)               0.00243 s ->       0.0011 km =    0.00001 deg of longitude
   ignore UT1-UTC, worst allowed (0.9 s)                                          0.90000 s ->       0.4186 km =    0.00376 deg of longitude
   use TAI instead of UTC (37 s, e.g. raw Cesium JulianDate.dayNumber/secondsOfDay)   37.00000 s ->      17.2087 km =    0.15459 deg of longitude
   use TT instead of UTC (69.184 s)                                              69.18400 s ->      32.1776 km =    0.28906 deg of longitude
   use local time CDT (UTC-5 h) instead of UTC                                 18000.00000 s ->    8371.8195 km =   75.20533 deg of longitude
   use local time CST (UTC-6 h) instead of UTC                                 21600.00000 s ->   10046.1834 km =   90.24640 deg of longitude
   JS Date millisecond rounding (0.0005 s)                                        0.00050 s ->       0.0002 km =    0.00000 deg of longitude
Contrast: the SAME 1 s error applied to the propagation epoch moves a LEO satellite ALONG-TRACK by v ~ 7.66 km/s * 1 s = 7.7 km,
          so a clock error hurts ~16x more through SGP4's tsince than through GMST.

7. ISS orbit numbers that depend on time conventions
==============================================================================
mean motion 15.48958602 rev/day -> period 1440/n = 92.966 min  (SOLAR minutes; TLE mean motion is per 86400 s day)
Earth turns 23.305 deg (sidereal rate 360.9856 deg/day) per orbit;  J2 nodal regression ~ -4.96 deg/day (westward)
-> successive ground tracks shift ~23.63 deg WEST per orbit (crude 360/16 = 22.5 deg is what the master doc quotes)
